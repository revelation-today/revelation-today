After chapter thirteen, relief: the Lamb standing on Mount Zion with that uncountable crowd,
singing a song only they can learn.

Their weapon is stated plainly, and it is not cleverness or strength. They stayed. They kept
trusting and never pretended not to know him.

That is the entire armoury. It is available to everyone, which is the point - nothing here
requires you to be remarkable.

Three angels fly past with three announcements. The first carries good news for every nation:
the offer is still open, to all. The second says Babylon has fallen. The third gives a serious
warning about following the beast.

Watch the order. The good news goes first. The warning is real, but the invitation leads.

Then the harvest, in two parts. The first is a gathering-in - people brought home, some of
them because they watched somebody stay faithful when it was costly. The second is a
winepress, for those who wanted nothing to do with the blood that was already shed.

Say it plainly and without relish: this is the grim half, and the book does not enjoy it
either.

Why two? Because a choice with no consequences is not a choice, it is a game. Fourteen
chapters of invitation, warning, sealing and protecting come first. Nobody reaches the
winepress by accident.
