This is Revelation for readers who have stopped expecting the world to get better.

Not readers who are grieving, and not readers under active persecution - there are other
versions here for both. This one is for the person who has looked at how things actually go,
drawn a conclusion, and found that hope now feels like something you talk yourself into.
People who were promised a turn that never came. People who watched the wrong side win and
kept watching. People who left a church that had an answer for everything except what was
happening in front of them.

The book was written to people with exactly that evidence. John's readers had watched Rome
kill the man they called King, and then go on running the world for sixty more years without
apparent interruption. Nothing in their experience suggested things were about to improve.
Revelation does not tell them they are wrong about what they can see. It tells them they are
looking at a smaller room than they think.

## What this version does differently

It does not open with reassurance. It stays with the parts of the book that other tellings
move past quickly, because those are the parts that answer this reader: the fifth seal, where
the question "how long?" gets no date in reply; chapter eleven, where God's witnesses are
killed and the world throws a party; chapter thirteen, where the beast wins visibly and
everyone follows it. Those are in the text. They are not a failure of nerve on the book's
part.

It also refuses the two cheap endings. It does not say the world is fine. It does not say the
world is disposable and you get evacuated. Revelation ends with God moving into a city that
human beings built out of fear, and staying.

## Production notes

Read at your own pace. Nothing here asks you to feel better by the end, and the honest answer
to "when" is the one the book itself gives, which is not a date. If you are using this with
someone, resist finishing their sentences toward optimism - this version is built to survive
being read by someone who does not believe it yet.
