Full Sunday-school lesson plans for teaching Revelation to children, in three age-tiered
tracks. Each session has an opening activity, the Bible story, an application, a memory
verse, a prayer, and an appendix of prep ideas — games, crafts, discussion questions and
song suggestions.

| Track | Ages | Sessions |
|---|---|---|
| [Early Childhood]({{< relref "kids/lessons/early-childhood" >}}) — *Jesus Wins, and He Loves Us* | 4–7 | 16 |
| [Elementary]({{< relref "kids/lessons/elementary" >}}) — *the core series* | 8–12 | 16 |
| [Teens]({{< relref "kids/lessons/teens" >}}) | 13–18 | 20 |

All three run straight through Revelation in five parts: the background and opening vision,
the seven churches, the throne room and what comes out of it, the great conflict, and the
ending. What changes between tracks is depth, not the story.

These are plans for **preparing and leading a group**, not pages for quiet personal reading.
