Segments come from .Ancestors, so section titles are already translated. Two things are suppressed: - grouping directories that carry no meaning for the reader ($skip below, plus any section with hideInTrail: true in front matter) - the current page itself, since its title is the H1 immediately beneath A position counter ("Lesson 2 of 16") is appended only for pages that declare audience_group, i.e. the kids lesson tracks, where the ordering is a genuine numbered course.
